How I work

Principles that guide every project

Consistency beats talent

Consistent effort towards your craft matters more than talent. I'm not the most talented designer in the world, but I strive for consistency and constant improvement — everyday.

Let curiosity guide you

Curiosity fuels my endless quest for understanding. I know a little about a lot of things — it helps me connect the dots between scattered information and create work that makes a difference.

Form follows function

Problems are solved with functionality, not aesthetics. But once the problem is solved, going the extra mile and designing beautiful products creates delight. Humans remember delight.

Give a damn

I'm highly entrepreneurial with a founder's mindset. Success is a shared responsibility. I take ownership for each project and go the extra mile to achieve it.
