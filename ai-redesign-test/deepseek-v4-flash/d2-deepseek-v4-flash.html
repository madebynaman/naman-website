/process

Six steps, no detours

This is what I actually do. Every step exists because skipping it has burned me before. No ceremony, just coverage.

01

Research

Understand the problem, the customer, the competition. Interviews, heuristics, competitive analysis. No assumptions.

02

User Journey

Map the experience. Find the seams, the drop-offs, the moments that matter. Personas, journey maps, decision points.

03

Design Exploration

Go wide. Multiple directions, rapid prototypes, collaborative critique. Pressure-test ideas before committing.

04

Refinement

Polish the chosen direction. Visual design, system thinking, handoff documentation. Every pixel intentional.

05

Handoff

Work with engineering, not throw over the wall. Close collaboration through implementation.

06

Monitor

Ship and measure. Design doesn't end at launch — track metrics, iterate, improve.
