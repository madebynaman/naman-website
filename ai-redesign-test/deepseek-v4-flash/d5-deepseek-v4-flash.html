My Process

Six phases. No shortcuts.

Each step earned its place through hard experience. Skipping any one has consequences.

01

Research

User interviews, competitive audits, heuristics. Understand before you act.

02

User Journey

Map the experience. Find friction points, drop-offs, and delight opportunities.

03

Explore

Go broad. Multiple directions, rapid prototypes, collaborative pressure-testing.

04

Refine

Polish the winner. Visual design, system thinking, handoff-ready documentation.

05

Handoff

Work with engineering. Close collaboration through implementation, not handover.

06

Monitor

Ship and measure. Real metrics tell you if you solved the right problem.
