Don't Uninstall Me Now

feat. Boost360 (NowFloats) · prod. Thence · 2021 · research / UX / usability testing

Users bailed within 4 hours; signup success sat at 20%. Twelve hypotheses tested down to two root causes — confusing signup and broken marketing promises. Rebuilt onboarding around a quick win: your business website, live in minutes.

Liner Notes

Naman Sharma is a self-taught Product/UX designer with a Computer Engineering degree and three years of UX on top of years of graphic design. He got into this because product design sits exactly where his obsessions overlap: creativity, problem-solving, technology.

The style: research first, always. He's interviewed users face to face in three cities, tested prototypes with real people, and treats a shipped design as the start of the work, not the end — because live isn't done until the metrics say so.

A curious generalist who has done field studies, web apps, mobile apps, logos, print, packaging and no-code builds — and is currently re-learning to code so ideas can go straight from head to production. Records from Jaipur; collaborates worldwide. No drama, easy to work with, gives a damn.

Four principles behind every record

Consistency beats talent

Consistent effort compounds into unimaginable results. Not the most talented designer alive — the one who shows up and improves every day.

Let curiosity guide you

Knowing a little about a lot is how scattered information becomes connected dots. Every project is a journey of discovery.

Form follows function

Problems are solved with functionality, not aesthetics. Solve the need first — then make it beautiful. Humans remember delight.

Give a damn

Founder's mindset, high agency, full ownership. Success is shared, and the extra mile is standard pressing quality.
