# AI Redesign Test: Gemini 3.1 Pro

This folder contains 5 distinct design directions for Naman Sharma's portfolio homepage.

## d1: Brutalist & Developer-Centric
**Concept:** A nod to Naman's computer science background and his principle of "Form follows function." It treats the portfolio like a technical document or blueprint.
**Key Design Decisions:** Used high-contrast black and white, heavy borders, visible grid structures, and a monospace typography paired with a clean sans-serif. Visual placeholders are presented as literal wireframe descriptions.
**Trade-offs Accepted:** Sacrifices warmth and traditional "delight" for a rigid, highly functional, and slightly aggressive aesthetic. It risks feeling too developer-focused for purely visual design roles.

## d2: Editorial / Quiet Luxury
**Concept:** Emphasizes the "beautiful products create delight" aspect of Naman's philosophy. It positions him as a premium, mature designer who cares about typography and intentionality.
**Key Design Decisions:** Implemented an asymmetrical layout with ample whitespace, elegant serif typography (Playfair Display), and a muted, earthy color palette (cream, charcoal, gold). 
**Trade-offs Accepted:** The low information density and generous whitespace mean the user must scroll more to consume the content. It might feel slightly too traditional or agency-like for fast-paced tech startups.

## d3: Vibrant Neo-Brutalism / Pop Art
**Concept:** Captures the "Curiosity" and "Give a damn" principles. It's highly energetic, memorable, and shows a strong, fearless creative agency.
**Key Design Decisions:** Utilized a stark CMYK-inspired palette (yellow, magenta, cyan), thick black borders, solid drop shadows, and a bold grotesque font (Space Grotesk). Included playful elements like a marquee ticker and slightly rotated containers.
**Trade-offs Accepted:** The extreme visual noise can distract from the actual case study content. It may alienate more conservative corporate clients who prefer safe, corporate minimalism.

## d4: Dark Mode Glassmorphism (Spatial Web)
**Concept:** Focuses on "Modern Tech" and Naman's role in building digital products from 0 to 1. It showcases high UI polish and technical proficiency.
**Key Design Decisions:** Used a deep dark background (Zinc 950) with subtle neon gradient orbs, frosted glass cards (backdrop-filter), and a clean, geometric sans-serif (Outfit). 
**Trade-offs Accepted:** Glassmorphism can present accessibility and contrast challenges. It is also a very trendy aesthetic that might age faster than a timeless editorial or modernist design.

## d5: Swiss / Bauhaus / Modernist
**Concept:** The purest expression of "Form follows function." It prioritizes structure, readability, and content over decorative elements.
**Key Design Decisions:** Enforced a strict 12-column grid, flush-left alignment everywhere, tight sans-serif typography (Inter), and a stark black/white scheme with sparse primary color accents (Red, Blue, Yellow).
**Trade-offs Accepted:** The rigid grid and lack of decorative elements can feel cold or overly clinical. It requires the actual content (which is currently placeholders) to carry the entire visual weight of the page.
