03 // SELECTED EXPERIMENTS

Case Studies

CASE_STUDY_01 // MULTI-PLATFORM DEALER PORTAL CLIENT: ASIAN PAINTS

Asian Paints Dealer Portal Revamp

✓ Impact: 20% increase in online transaction volume

Redesigned the supply chain tracking and order placement experience for regional distributors, reducing dependency on on-field salesmen and accelerating orders.

  • Research finding: Dealers are frustrated with existing digital options, requiring simple, clean layout abstractions with custom dealer/staff permissions.
  • Strategy: Introduced 10-second barcode ordering, intelligent product recommendation blocks, and size-based incentives directly in the portal.
  • Role: UX Research, UX/UI Design, Collaborated with PM, Design Lead & Client Team.

CASE_STUDY_02 // APP ONBOARDING SYSTEM CLIENT: BOOST360 BY NOWFLOATS

Boost360 Onboarding Redesign

✓ Impact: 60% decrease in app uninstall rate within first 2 weeks

Identified friction leading to immediate app uninstalls. Streamlined onboarding to visual steps and resolved marketing expectation mismatches.

  • Research finding: Users experienced a disconnect between marketing advertisements and the technical app interface.
  • Strategy: Designed visual progress indicators, highlighted immediate value propositions, and enabled instant site creation during setup.
  • Role: UX Research, UX/UI Design, Usability Testing, Motion Design collaboration.

CASE_STUDY_03 // SMART FEATURE PHONE UX AUDIT CLIENT: WIKIMEDIA FOUNDATION

Wikipedia KaiOS/JioPhone Research

✓ Scope: 25 In-depth Field Interviews across Pune, Mumbai, Delhi

Uncovered core internet adoption, reading behaviors, and feature requirements for smart feature phone users navigating the KaiOS ecosystem.

  • Insight: Key motivations for JioPhone are cheap repairs and battery life. Users struggle with dense text formatting on small screens.
  • Recommendations: Implement Voice User Interfaces (VUI), integrate localized English-numeral options, and optimize table layouts.
  • Role: UX Research Intern (Hureo), User Persona Synthesis, Journey Mapping.
