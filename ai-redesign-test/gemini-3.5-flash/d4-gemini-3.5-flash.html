    02 // Core Design Thesis

    Consistency > Talent

    Consistent daily efforts compound into major design successes. I prioritize routine skill development and systematic execution over isolated inspiration.

    Curious Generalist

    A background in CS and interest in personal finance, gaming, and code allows me to understand developer boundaries and connect distant logical dots.

    Form Follows Function

    If a product is not functional, visual polish is useless. But when mechanics are optimized, high-fidelity delight creates memorable user loyalty.

    High Agency

    Holding a founder's mindset. I reject rigid handoff boundaries and collaborate actively with engineering and product to ensure layout integrity.
