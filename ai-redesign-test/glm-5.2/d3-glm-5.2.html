Abstract — what this sheet is.

SCALE 1:1

I design products that users love, for brands that care. That's it.

Self-taught Product / UX designer — three years in UX on top of a computer-science background. Working remotely from Jaipur, India, for start-ups building 0→1 and for enterprises that want UX taken seriously.

The work is research-rooted and built to be measured. This sheet is drawn the same way the work is done: state the problem, show the method, measure the outcome. Form follows function — the drawing is honest because the problem was solved before the page was made pretty.

DETAIL D.01 · NS/AP/2021

Asian Paints
dealer portal

Agency

Year

Role
UX research · UX/UI
With

enterprisesupply chainAOV

How might we improve the dealer experience — digitizing order placement and supply-chain tracking — while increasing transaction volumes and AOV?

Dealers didn't want to call a salesperson for every order. Every competitor already had a portal, and dealers were uniformly frustrated with all of them. Role-based abstraction was needed for users with varying privileges, and Asian Paints held demand data it could use to nudge dealers intelligently.

  • Heuristic evaluation and end-to-end redesign of the existing portal.
  • Personalized product recommendations from each dealer's order history.
  • Order-size incentives and loyalty points to lift AOV; data-driven regional promotions.
  • Order a product in under 10 seconds by scanning its barcode.

DETAIL D.02 · NS/B360/2021

Boost360
onboarding

Agency
Thence
Year
2021
Role
UX research · UX/UI · usability
With
UI · motion · PM · DM · client
mobilefunnel fixonboarding

New users were uninstalling before they signed up. How might we reduce the uninstall rate and push users to complete onboarding, try the app, and stay?

Signup success sat near 20%. Time to signup averaged ~30 minutes. Eleven hypotheses — crashes, OTP delays, ad-driven expectation mismatch. Research narrowed it: the signup UX was poor, and marketing had promised something the app didn't quite deliver.

  • Made signup visual so the process reads at a glance.
  • Communicated real USPs during onboarding; built trust with social proof.
  • Created quick wins so users see results fast; fixed screen-level UX issues.
  • Aligned marketing communication with reality across every touchpoint.

DETAIL D.03 · NS/WIK/2020

Wikipedia
KaiOS research

Agency
Hureo
Year
2020
Role
UX research · personas · journey
With
UX researchers · founder
field research25 pax3 cities

Understand JioPhone users — a smart feature phone on KaiOS — and how they read, search, and understand Wikipedia on a small keypad screen.

One-on-one, in-person interviews in Pune, Mumbai, and Delhi. 25 participants — students, business owners, homemakers, bank employees, salesmen, field executives, 18–40. 45–60 minutes each: 30–45 on background and habits, then observed task-based usage on their own devices.

  • Why JioPhone: smart features at affordable price, family Jio sims, cheap to repair, durable, familiar keypad, strong battery.
  • Wishlist: more images and video, English numerals in Hindi articles, in-article TOC, in-article search, latest-first ordering, bigger fonts and more colour.
  • Gap: users didn't know they could search inside Wikipedia, or that it existed in regional languages.
