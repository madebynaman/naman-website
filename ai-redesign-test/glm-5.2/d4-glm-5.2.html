01 · The thesis

I design products that users love, for brands that care. That's it. The rest is execution — and giving a damn.

Self-taught Product / UX designer — three years in UX on top of a computer-science background. Working remotely from Jaipur, India, for start-ups building 0→1 and enterprises that want UX taken seriously.

The work is research-rooted and built to be measured. Improving people's lives with design and tech, one pixel at a time — and owning the outcome, not just the deliverable.

I hate jargon. I don't synergize aesthetics and functionality, orchestrate design revolutions, or perpetuate narratives that transcend boundaries. — author's note

Asian Paints
dealer portal

Thence2021
Role: UX research · UX/UI With: UI · PM · design mgr · client Type: enterprise · supply chain

How might we improve the dealer experience — digitizing order placement and supply-chain tracking — while increasing transaction volumes and AOV?

Dealers didn't want to call a salesperson for every order. Every competitor already had a portal, and dealers were uniformly frustrated with all of them. The portal needed role-based abstraction for users with varying privileges, and Asian Paints held demand data it could use to nudge dealers intelligently.

  • Heuristic evaluation and end-to-end redesign of the existing portal.
  • Personalized product recommendations from each dealer's order history.
  • Order-size incentives and loyalty points to lift AOV; data-driven regional promotions.
  • Order a product in under 10 seconds by scanning its barcode.

Signup success sat near 20%. Time to signup averaged ~30 minutes. Eleven hypotheses — crashes, OTP delays, ad-driven expectation mismatch. Research narrowed it: the signup UX was poor, and marketing had promised something the app didn't quite deliver.

  • Made signup visual so the process reads at a glance.

03

Wikipedia
KaiOS research

Hureo2020
Role: UX research · personas · journey mapping With: UX researchers · founder Type: field research · 25 participants · 3 cities

Understand JioPhone users — a smart feature phone on KaiOS — and how they read, search, and understand Wikipedia on a small keypad screen.

One-on-one, in-person interviews in Pune, Mumbai, and Delhi. 25 participants — students, business owners, homemakers, bank employees, salesmen, field executives, 18–40. 45–60 minutes each: 30–45 on background and habits, then observed task-based usage on their own devices.

  • Why JioPhone: smart features at affordable price, family Jio sims, cheap to repair, durable, familiar keypad, strong battery.
  • Wishlist: more images and video, English numerals in Hindi articles, in-article TOC, in-article search, latest-first ordering, bigger fonts.
  • Gap: users didn't know they could search inside Wikipedia, or that it existed in regional languages.
