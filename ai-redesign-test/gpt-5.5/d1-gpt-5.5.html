Process

  1. Research the problem and the customer.
  2. Map the user journey and decision points.
  3. Explore solutions with product and engineering.
  4. Refine the chosen direction into polished UI.
  5. Handoff with documentation and close collaboration.
  6. Monitor live performance and improve.
