Principles

The working voice is plain on purpose.

No jargon is not an aesthetic preference. It is a product habit: say what is happening, make the next action clear, and let the work prove the point.

Consistency beats talent

Craft compounds through daily effort and constant improvement.

Let curiosity guide you

Range helps connect research, technology, visual design, and business context.

Form follows function

Good products solve needs first; delight makes the solution memorable.

Give a damn

Founder-minded ownership means success is shared and details matter.
