03 / Working method

A loop, not a ceremony.

Research

Learn the customer and the context.

Journey

Map decisions, needs, and friction.

Explore

Test multiple plausible answers.

Refine

Make the chosen direction coherent.

Handoff

Partner through implementation.

Monitor

Measure reality and improve again.
