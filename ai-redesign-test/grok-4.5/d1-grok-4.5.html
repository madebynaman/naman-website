Asian Paints — Dealer Portal

How might we digitize order placement and supply-chain tracking so dealers place more, larger orders without calling sales every time?

Heuristic evaluation and end-to-end redesign: personalized recommendations, loyalty nudges, geo-demand promotions, barcode reorder under 10 seconds, role-based privileges.

+20% online transaction volume Engagement and loyalty lifted with the new ordering loops.

Boost360 — Onboarding

How might we stop new installs from vanishing before signup when uninstalls hit within 4 hours and signup success sat at 20%?

Root cause: marketing promise vs. product reality. Redesigned for visual signup, USP clarity, social proof, quick website-creation wins, and optional skip paths. Usability testing confirmed users finally “got” the app.

Up to 60% fewer uninstalls Average time to successful signup dropped significantly.

Wikipedia — KaiOS User Research

Who are JioPhone users, how do they use the internet, and what would make Wikipedia useful on a smart feature phone?

25 in-person interviews across Pune, Mumbai, and Delhi. Mapped motivations (price, durability, keypad comfort, battery) and a wishlist from voice search to simpler Hindi and better JioPhone UI. Delivered recommendations for Wikimedia Foundation.

Operating beliefs

Principles that survive contact with a real product

Not manifesto wallpaper — filters for how decisions get made under constraint.

P01

Consistency beats talent

Daily craft compounds. I’m not chasing “most talented” — I’m chasing reliable improvement that shows up in the work.

P02

Let curiosity guide you

Knowing a little about a lot connects scattered signals. Curiosity is how research turns into products people actually need.

P03

Form follows function

Solve the job first. Then go the extra mile for beauty — humans remember delight after the problem is gone.

P04

Give a damn

Founder mindset on every engagement. High agency, shared responsibility, ownership past the handoff slide.

Field protocol

How a problem becomes a shipped surface

Six stages from understanding to monitoring. Order matters; skipping research is how uninstall rates happen.

STEP 01

Research

Problem space, possible solutions, and the customer as they actually behave.

STEP 02

User journey

Personas, journeys, and decision points where friction or drop-off hides.

STEP 03

Design exploration

Go wide. Test, iterate, collaborate through the real constraints.

STEP 04

Design refinement

Choose a direction. Polish visual design, document decisions, prepare handoff.

STEP 05

Handoff

Partner with engineering and product so implementation matches intent.

STEP 06

Monitoring

Live is not done. Track the metrics that prove success — or demand the next pass.
