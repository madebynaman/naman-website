Process

How the work actually happens

  1. Research

    Understand the problem, options, and customer.

  2. User Journey

    Personas, journeys, decision-making.

  3. Design Exploration

    Go wide. Test. Iterate. Collaborate.

  4. Design Refinement

    Polish visuals. Document decisions. Prep handoff.

  5. Handoff

    Work with engineering and product so it ships right.

  6. Monitoring

    Live isn’t done. Track the metrics that mean success.
