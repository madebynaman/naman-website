Gallery two

Principles

Beliefs that stay on the wall when the brief gets difficult.

Plaque I

Consistency beats talent

Daily craft compounds. I’m not the most talented designer in the room — I am the one who keeps improving.

Plaque II

Let curiosity guide you

Knowing a little about a lot connects scattered information. Curiosity is how work becomes useful.

Plaque III

Form follows function

Problems are solved with function first. Beauty is the extra mile that makes humans remember delight.

Plaque IV

Give a damn

Founder mindset. High agency. Own the project past the handoff — success is shared responsibility.

Method room

Process

Six stages from understanding to monitoring. Sequence is the method.

Stage 01

Research

Problem space, possible solutions, and the customer as they behave.

Stage 02

User journey

Personas, paths, and decision points where drop-off hides.

Stage 03

Exploration

Go wide. Test, iterate, collaborate through real constraints.

Stage 04

Refinement

Choose a direction. Polish visual design. Document decisions.

Stage 05

Handoff

Partner with engineering and product so intent survives build.

Stage 06

Monitoring

Live is not done. Track the metrics that prove success.
