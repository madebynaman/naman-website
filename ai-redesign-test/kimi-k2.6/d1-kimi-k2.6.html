Method

Process

No two projects follow the exact same path, but every project starts with research and ends with monitoring. This is the backbone.

01

Research

Understanding the problem, considering possible solutions, and learning about the customer. I don't start drawing until I know who I'm drawing for.

02

User Journey

Diving deep into the user journey to uncover insights. Exploring personas, journeys, and understanding decision making at every touchpoint.

03

Design Exploration

Going wide and exploring a range of possible solutions. Testing, iterating, collaborating, and working through the problem at hand without premature convergence.

04

Design Refinement

Refining a chosen direction and polishing visual design. Preparing for handoff and documenting the decisions made along the way.

05

Handoff

Working closely with engineering and product to ensure successful implementation. A design is only as good as its execution.

06

Monitoring

Once live, the work isn't done. Monitoring performance and tracking metrics that indicate success — and finding the next thing to improve.

Beliefs

Principles

These aren't posters on a wall. They are heuristics I use when I'm stuck, when the path forward is unclear, or when I need to decide what matters.

Consistency beats talent

I'm not the most talented designer in the world, but I strive for consistency and constant improvement — everyday. Consistency compounds into unimaginable results.

Let curiosity guide you

Curiosity fuels my endless quest for understanding. I know a little about a lot of things — it helps me connect the dots between scattered information.

Form follows function

Problems are solved with functionality, not aesthetics. But once the problem is solved, going the extra mile and designing beautiful products creates delight. And humans remember delight.

Give a damn

I'm highly entrepreneurial and strive to have a founder's mindset. Success is a shared responsibility. I take ownership for each project I work on.
