Workflow Map

Process

No two projects follow the same path, but every project starts with understanding and ends with measurement. Here's the map.

01

Research

Understand the problem, consider solutions, learn about the customer.

02

User Journey

Dive deep into personas, journeys, and decision-making.

03

Design Exploration

Go wide. Test, iterate, collaborate. Don't converge too early.

04

Design Refinement

Polish the chosen direction. Prepare for handoff. Document decisions.

05

Handoff

Work closely with engineering. A design is only as good as its execution.

06

Monitoring

Track metrics. Find the next thing to improve. The work is never done.

The Wall — Principles

Principles

Pinned up where I can see them. Heuristics for when the path forward is unclear.

Consistency beats talent

I'm not the most talented designer in the world, but I show up every day. Consistency compounds into unimaginable results.

Let curiosity guide you

Knowing a little about a lot helps me connect the dots. Each project is a journey of discovery, constant learning, and exploration.

Form follows function

Problems are solved with functionality, not aesthetics. But once solved, going the extra mile creates delight. Humans remember delight.

Give a damn

Founder's mindset. Ownership of outcomes, not just outputs. Success is a shared responsibility that requires agency and commitment.
