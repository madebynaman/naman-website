# Design Plan — Kimi k2.6

## Direction 1: "Field Notes" — Editorial, warm, tactile
- **Concept:** A UX researcher's field notebook. Warm paper textures, margin annotations, stamped dates, pinned observations.
- **Palette:** Parchment `#F5F0E8`, Ink `#1A1A1A`, Terracotta `#C4704A`, Moss `#4A6741`, Graphite `#6B6B6B`, Highlight `#E8DCC4`
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (display/headings) + Inter (body) + JetBrains Mono (labels/captions)
- **Layout:** Asymmetric editorial grid, generous whitespace with dense info clusters. Left-margin annotations on desktop.
- **Signature:** CSS "pinned note" cards with rotation and tape effects, margin annotations for section labels, stamped date markers.
- **Content sections:** Hero (field-notebook cover), Case Studies (pinned observations), Process (numbered research steps), Principles (margin notes), Timeline (stamped dates), Contact (closing note)

## Direction 2: "System" — Brutalist-leaning, engineering-minded
- **Concept:** A well-designed OS / design system. CS background meets design craft. Strict grid, monospace, utilitarian beauty.
- **Palette:** Paper `#FAFAFA`, Ink `#0A0A0A`, Signal Cyan `#00B4D8`, Muted `#8D8D8D`, Border `#E0E0E0`
- **Type:** Space Grotesk (display) + IBM Plex Mono (body/labels) + Inter (reading)
- **Layout:** Dense newspaper-like columns, border-heavy, zero border-radius, systematic hierarchy through weight/size.
- **Signature:** Command-line typed intro, systematic section numbering that encodes career progression, CSS "terminal window" case-study frames.
- **Content sections:** Hero (typing command), Selected Work (system modules), Process (system flow diagram), Principles (spec sheet), Timeline (log entries), Contact (command prompt)

## Direction 3: "Growth" — Fluid, organic, maximalist
- **Concept:** Design as living, growing thing. Organic shapes, generative gradients, nature-inspired.
- **Palette:** Sage `#E8F3E8`, Forest `#2D4A3E`, Coral `#E07A5F`, Sand `#F2E8CF`, Midnight `#1D2D35`, Mist `#D4E4D4`
- **Type:** Playfair Display (display) + DM Sans (body) + Space Mono (accents)
- **Layout:** Breaking grid, floating elements, scroll-driven section transitions, generous curves.
- **Signature:** Living CSS gradient blobs that shift on scroll, organic container shapes with border-radius morphing.
- **Content sections:** Hero (greeting from nature), Work (garden of projects), Process (growth rings), Principles (roots), Timeline (growth chart), Contact (seed packet)

## Direction 4: "Studio Wall" — Collage, mixed-media, creative energy
- **Concept:** A physical studio wall. Naman the generalist — everything pinned up, overlapping, alive with creative energy.
- **Palette:** Warm White `#FDFCF8`, Charcoal `#2C2C2C`, Ochre `#D4A373`, Slate `#6B7D8B`, Cream `#F5F1E8`, Post-it `#F4E285`
- **Type:** Syne (display) + Source Serif 4 (body) + DM Mono (accents)
- **Layout:** Masonry-like pinned board, overlapping cards, varied sizes, layered shadows.
- **Signature:** CSS "pinned" elements with slight rotations, layered shadows, tape/clip effects, sticky notes, polaroid frames.
- **Content sections:** Hero (welcome to the studio), Selected Work (pinned projects), Process (workflow map), Principles (sticky notes), Timeline (photo strip), Contact (business card)

## Direction 5: "Sumi" — Minimalist, Japanese-inspired restraint
- **Concept:** Form follows function. Extreme restraint. Every element earns its place. Haiku-like precision.
- **Palette:** Rice Paper `#FEFDFB`, Sumi Ink `#1C1C1C`, Vermillion `#E34234`, Stone `#B8B0A8`, Washi `#F2EFE9`
- **Type:** Noto Serif JP (display — used for character/weight) + Inter (body) + JetBrains Mono (data)
- **Layout:** Centered narrow column, vast whitespace, precise vertical rhythm, hairline rules, deliberate asymmetry.
- **Signature:** Single continuous vertical brush-stroke line connecting sections, scroll-driven drawing animation.
- **Content sections:** Hero (single statement), Work (breathing room), Process (empty space between steps), Principles (one per screen), Timeline (vertical ink line), Contact (red seal)
