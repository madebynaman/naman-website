Process

How I Work
01

Research

Understanding the problem space, the customer, and the possible solutions. Every decision starts with evidence.

02

User Journey

Diving deep into personas, journeys, and decision-making patterns to uncover the insights that matter.

03

Design Exploration

Going wide. Testing, iterating, collaborating. Exploring the full range before committing to a direction.

04

Refinement

Polishing the chosen direction. Preparing designs for handoff. Documenting every decision along the way.

05

Handoff

Working closely with engineering and product to ensure successful implementation.

06

Monitoring

Live doesn't mean done. Tracking metrics, monitoring performance, and iterating based on real data.

About

Principles

Curious generalist.
Self-taught.

Product/UX design sits at the intersection of my passion for creativity, problem solving, and technology. I'm a curious generalist — whether it's UX research, usability testing, designing apps, websites, logos, or product packaging, I've done it all.

I hate jargon. I don't "foster innovation through synergizing aesthetics and functionality." I solve problems. I make things easy to use. I go the extra mile because I give a damn.

Consistency beats talent

Consistent efforts compound into unimaginable results. I'm not the most talented designer — but I strive for improvement every day.

Let curiosity guide you

I know a little about a lot. It helps me connect dots between scattered information and create work that actually makes a difference.

Form follows function

Problems are solved with functionality, not aesthetics. But going the extra mile to design beautiful products creates delight. And humans remember delight.

Give a damn

Entrepreneurial mindset. High agency. I take ownership and commit to going the extra mile — because success is a shared responsibility.
