Case 01

Asian Paints — Dealer Portal

Thence · 2021 · Research · UX · UI
12 screens · end-to-end Web · Desktop

The problem

Asian Paints dealers were calling sales reps to place every order. Competitor portals existed; dealers hated all of them. Transaction volumes and AOV were flat, and the brief was simple: digitize ordering, lift AOV, and make the portal worth using.

What I did

Heuristic evaluation, then an end-to-end redesign of twelve screens — Home, Trending, Categories, Catalog, Listing, Order, Stock, Wishlist, Cart, Tracking, Account, Reports. Added barcode-scan ordering, personalized recommendations from history, loyalty points, and order-size incentives to nudge AOV.

What came out

A +20% lift in online transaction volume, and a healthy increase in user engagement and loyalty. Salesman–dealer interaction dropped. The portal started carrying its weight.

+20% Online transaction volume ↑ Engagement & loyalty 12 screens shipped
Case 02

Boost360 — Onboarding

Thence · 2021 · Research · UX · UI · Usability
Onboarding · 3 states iOS · Android

The problem

80% of new Boost360 users uninstalled the app before signing up. Average time-to-signup was 30 minutes. Something between the marketing and the product was breaking.

What I found

It wasn't crashes, OTP, ads, or permissions. It was a mismatch — between what the marketing promised and what onboarding delivered. A UX problem hiding inside a positioning problem.

What I did

Made signup visual. Communicated USPs during onboarding, not after. Added social proof for trust. Created a quick win before the form fields. Routed users to support if they got stuck. Ran usability tests on every iteration.

−60% 14-day uninstall rate ↓ Time-to-signup 4 states

The brief

Wikimedia wanted to understand JioPhone (KaiOS) users — people who chose a feature phone over a smartphone, and what that meant for Wikipedia on their device.

What I did

25 in-person interviews, 45–60 minutes each, across Pune, Mumbai, and Delhi. Recruited participants, ran the sessions, transcribed, built personas, mapped journeys, and produced a research report with concrete recommendations.

What came out

Voice-first interaction. Readability for small screens. Video content. Search visibility. Regional-language SEO. Simpler Hindi. The findings shaped how Wikipedia approached KaiOS for the year that followed.

25 in-person interviews 3 cities ~60m per session

Section 03

Four working beliefs.

Short on purpose
01

Consistency beats talent.

I'm not the most talented designer in the room. I am, reliably, in the room. Consistent effort compounds into results that look like talent in hindsight.

02

Let curiosity guide you.

I know a little about a lot of things, and it helps me connect dots between scattered information. Each project is a journey of discovery.

03

Form follows function.

Problems are solved with functionality, not aesthetics. Once the problem is solved, going the extra mile on the visual layer is what creates delight.

04

Give a damn.

I work with a founder's mindset. I take ownership of outcomes, not deliverables. The project's success is my success while it's mine.
