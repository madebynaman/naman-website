Feature 02 · Build · Thence · 2021

BUILDS.

Asian Paints — Dealer Portal
12 screens · end-to-end

The problem

Dealers were calling sales reps to place every order. Competitor portals existed; dealers hated all of them. Transaction volumes and AOV were flat.

The work

End-to-end redesign of twelve screens. Barcode-scan ordering. Personalized recommendations from history. Loyalty points. Order-size incentives. Heuristic evaluation first, then build.

The result

A +20% lift in online transaction volume. Healthy increase in engagement and loyalty. Salesman–dealer interaction dropped.

ON—BOARD.

The uninstall wasn't a bug. It was a promise.

Boost360 — Onboarding flow

The setup

80% of new Boost360 users uninstalled the app before signing up. Average time-to-signup: 30 minutes.

A UX problem hiding inside a positioning problem.

What I found

Not crashes. Not OTP. Not permissions. A mismatch between what marketing promised and what onboarding delivered.

Made signup visual. Communicated USPs during onboarding, not after. Added social proof. Created a quick win before the form. Ran usability tests on every iteration.

The study

Wikimedia wanted to understand the people using Wikipedia on JioPhones — a smart feature phone on KaiOS. The participants were not smartphone users by choice. That choice was the whole question.

We ran 25 in-person interviews across Pune, Mumbai, and Delhi. 45–60 minutes each. We sat with people in their homes, watched them use their phones, and asked why. We built personas, mapped journeys, and produced a research report with concrete recommendations.

The findings shaped how Wikipedia approached KaiOS for the year that followed. Voice-first interaction. Readability for small screens. Video content. Better search visibility. Regional-language SEO. Simpler Hindi.
