Exhibit A — Selected Case Files
Case #001 — Thence / 2021
case-study hero — Asian Paints dealer portal

Asian Paints: Dealer Portal

UX Research · UX Design · UI Design

Dealers were frustrated with digital portals. Every competitor had one, and all of them were painful. The investigation revealed dealers didn't want to call sales reps for every order — they wanted self-service with intelligence.

Redesigned the end-to-end multi-platform dealer portal with personalized product recommendations, barcode scanning for 10-second orders, and data-driven promotions based on geographical trends.

20% increase in online transaction volume
Case #002 — Thence / 2021
case-study hero — Boost360 onboarding flow

Boost360: Onboarding Redesign

UX Research · UX Design · Usability Testing

New users were uninstalling within 4 hours. Signup success rate was 20% with a 30-minute completion time. The hypothesis list was long — app crashes, OTP delays, too many permissions.

The real culprit? A mismatch between marketing promises and onboarding reality. Made signup visual, communicated USPs during onboarding, and created quick wins so users saw results immediately.

60% decrease in app uninstall rate
Case #003 — Hureo / 2020
research artifact — Wikipedia KaiOS user study

Wikipedia: KaiOS User Research

UX Research · User Personas · Journey Mapping

The Wikimedia Foundation needed to understand JioPhone users — people using smart feature phones running KaiOS. 25 in-person interviews across Pune, Mumbai, and Delhi. 45–60 minute sessions each.

Uncovered that users wanted voice-based interaction, video-format content, and optimized readability for small screens. Users preferred Hindi articles with simpler words and English numerals for dates.

Research informing Wikipedia's KaiOS strategy
Case #004 — Freelance / 2022–23
project mockup — Turns landing page & brand

Turns: Brand & Landing Pages

Web Design · Brand Guidelines · Design System

A startup needed more than a prettier website — they needed a multi-platform brand identity and a design system that could scale. Conducted a full UX audit of existing touchpoints.

Redesigned the website and landing pages, established brand guidelines and a component design system. The revamp directly increased conversions and became part of the investor pitch.
