Operating Principles

What I Believe

01

Consistency beats talent

Consistent effort towards the craft compounds into unimaginable results. Not the most talented — but striving for constant improvement, every day.

02

Let curiosity guide you

Knowing a little about a lot of things connects dots between scattered information. Each project is a journey of discovery, learning, and exploration.

03

Form follows function

Problems are solved with functionality. But once solved, going the extra mile to design beautiful products creates delight. Humans remember delight.

04

Give a damn

Entrepreneurial with a founder's mindset. High agency, full ownership, committed to going the extra mile. Success is a shared responsibility.
