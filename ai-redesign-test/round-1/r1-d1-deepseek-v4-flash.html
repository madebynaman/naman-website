How I Work

A process that bends toward outcomes

Every project is different, but the loop is the same: understand, explore, build, measure. I don't do process for process's sake — each step here exists because skipping it has cost me before.

Research

Understanding the problem, the customer, and the landscape before touching a tool. Competitive analysis, user interviews, heuristics.

User Journey

Mapping the experience to find the seams. Personas, journey maps, decision points — finding where the friction lives.

Design Exploration

Going wide before going deep. Multiple directions, rapid prototyping, collaborative critique.

Refinement

Polishing the chosen direction. Visual design, design systems, handoff documentation.

Handoff & Monitor

Close collaboration with engineering, then tracking metrics post-launch. Design doesn't end at handoff.
