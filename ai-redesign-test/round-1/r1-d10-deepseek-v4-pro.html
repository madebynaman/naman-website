Process

Six stages. Every project moves through this cycle — not always linearly, but always completely. The same rigor whether the canvas is a B2B portal or a consumer onboarding flow.

01
Research
Understanding the problem, the users, the competitive landscape. User interviews, heuristic evaluation, competitive analysis.
02
User Journey
Diving deep into journeys, mapping personas, uncovering the insights that shape the solution.
03
Design Exploration
Going wide. Multiple solution paths, rapid prototyping, testing, iterating with product and engineering.
04
Design Refinement
Polishing the chosen direction. Visual design, documentation, preparing every screen for handoff.
05
Handoff
Working alongside engineering to ensure the design is implemented as intended.
06
Monitoring
Tracking metrics post-launch to verify impact. Shipping is the midpoint.
Operating Principles

Four principles that guide the work when the brief is ambiguous and the stakes are real.

Consistency beats talent.
Consistent effort always compounds into unimaginable results. I strive for consistency and constant improvement — everyday. It's the single variable I can control.
Form follows function.
Problems are solved with functionality, not aesthetics. But once the problem is solved, going the extra mile and designing beautiful products creates delight. Humans remember delight.
Let curiosity guide you.
I know a little about a lot of things. It helps me connect dots between scattered information. Every project is a journey of discovery and learning.
Give a damn.
Founder's mindset: take ownership, collaborate effectively, go the extra mile. Success is a shared responsibility.
