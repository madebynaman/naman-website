I don't start drawing until I know who I'm drawing for. Research grounds every decision. User journeys uncover the real insights. Exploration goes wide before narrowing. Refinement polishes the chosen direction. Handoff ensures the design ships as intended. Monitoring closes the loop.

This isn't a rigid waterfall. It's a system — the nodes rewire based on the project. But the sequence is invariant: understand first, build second, measure last.

SYS.03

Principles

Design heuristics. Constraints that shape decisions when the path forward is unclear.

Constraint_01

Consistency beats talent

Compounding daily effort outperforms sporadic genius. I optimize for showing up, iterating, and measuring improvement over time.

Constraint_02

Let curiosity guide you

Generalist knowledge connects dots specialists miss. Each project is a journey of discovery, constant learning, and exploration.

Constraint_03

Form follows function

Functionality solves problems; aesthetics create delight. Solve first, beautify second. Humans remember delight.

Constraint_04

Give a damn

Founder's mindset. Ownership of outcomes, not just outputs. Success is a shared responsibility that requires agency and commitment.
