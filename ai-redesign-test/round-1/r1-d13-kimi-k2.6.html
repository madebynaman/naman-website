Growth Rings

Process

Like the rings of a tree, each phase adds a layer. The center is research. The outer bark is monitoring. Everything in between is craft.

1

Research

Understanding the problem, considering possible solutions, and learning about the customer. The seed won't sprout in the wrong soil.

2

User Journey

Diving deep into the user journey to uncover insights. Exploring personas, journeys, and understanding decision making at every fork.

3

Design Exploration

Going wide and exploring a range of possible solutions. Testing, iterating, collaborating, and working through the problem without premature convergence.

4

Design Refinement

Refining a chosen direction and polishing visual design. Preparing for handoff and documenting the decisions made along the way.

5

Handoff

Working closely with engineering and product to ensure successful implementation. A design is only as good as its execution.

6

Monitoring

Once live, the work isn't done. Monitoring performance and tracking metrics that indicate success — and finding the next thing to improve.

Root System

Principles

The invisible structure beneath every visible decision. These aren't slogans — they are heuristics I use when stuck.

Consistency beats talent

Compounding daily effort outperforms sporadic genius. I'm not the most talented designer in the world, but I show up every day.

Let curiosity guide you

Knowing a little about a lot helps me connect the dots between scattered information. Each project is a journey of discovery.

Form follows function

Problems are solved with functionality, not aesthetics. But once solved, going the extra mile creates delight. Humans remember delight.

Give a damn

I'm highly entrepreneurial with a founder's mindset. Success is a shared responsibility that requires ownership and agency.
