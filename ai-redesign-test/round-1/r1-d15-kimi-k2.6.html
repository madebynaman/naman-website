Process

Research

Understanding the problem, considering possible solutions, and learning about the customer. I do not start drawing until I know who I am drawing for.

User Journey

Diving deep into the user journey to uncover insights. Exploring personas, journeys, and understanding decision making.

Design Exploration

Going wide and exploring a range of possible solutions. Testing, iterating, collaborating, and working through the problem at hand.

Design Refinement

Refining a chosen direction and polishing visual design. Preparing for handoff and documenting the decisions made.

Handoff

Working closely with engineering and product to ensure successful implementation. A design is only as good as its execution.

Monitoring

Once a project is live, the work is not done. Monitoring performance and tracking metrics that indicate success.

Principles

Consistency beats talent

Consistent efforts towards your craft are more important than talent. I am not the most talented designer in the world, but I strive for consistency and constant improvement — everyday. Consistency always compounds into unimaginable results.

Let curiosity guide you

Curiosity fuels my endless quest for understanding. I know a little about a lot of things — it helps me connect the dots between scattered information. Each project is a journey of discovery, constant learning, and exploration.

Form follows function

Problems are solved with functionality, not aesthetics. At the core of every good product is how it solves for the needs of its users. But once the problem is solved, going the extra mile and designing beautiful products creates delight. And humans remember delight.

Give a damn

I am highly entrepreneurial and strive to have a founder's mindset. Success is a shared responsibility that is an outcome of effective collaboration. With a high level of agency, I take ownership for each project I work on.
