Exhibits — selected casework

Four representative engagements, 2020–2023. Each exhibit summarises the problem, the investigation, and the measured result. Full case studies run much deeper — research plans, hypothesis lists, testing notes, final screens.

Exhibit AThence · 2021

Asian Paints — Dealer Portal

UX Research · UX Design · UI Design

Dealers didn't want to call a salesperson for every order — and every digital portal in the category frustrated them. We ran a heuristic evaluation, then redesigned the portal end to end: barcode ordering in under 10 seconds, personalised recommendations from order history, and data-driven promotions by region.

Exhibit BThence · 2021

Boost360 — Onboarding

UX Research · UX Design · Usability Testing

Users were uninstalling within four hours; signup success sat at 20%. We listed twelve hypotheses, tested them, and found the real culprits: a confusing signup and a gap between marketing promises and the app. The redesign made onboarding visual, built trust with social proof, and engineered a quick win — your business website, live in minutes.

§04

Methodology

The same investigation, every time — because problems are solved with functionality, not aesthetics. Aesthetics come after, and they come hard.

Research

Understand the problem, the customer, and the possible solutions before anything is drawn.

User Journey

Map personas, journeys and decision-making to find the insights that actually matter.

Design Exploration

Go wide. Test, iterate, collaborate — work through the problem, not around it.

Design Refinement

Polish the chosen direction, document decisions, prepare for handoff.

Handoff

Work shoulder-to-shoulder with engineering and product so what ships matches what was designed.

Monitoring

Live isn't done. Track the metrics that define success and keep improving.

§05

Operating principles

Consistency beats talent

Consistent effort compounds into unimaginable results. Not the most talented designer in the world — just the one who shows up and improves, every day.

the compounding is the point

Let curiosity guide you

Knowing a little about a lot of things is how scattered information becomes connected dots. Every project is a journey of discovery.

Form follows function

Solve the need first. Then go the extra mile and make it beautiful — because humans remember delight.

Give a damn

Founder's mindset, high agency, shared responsibility. Ownership of every project, committed to the extra mile.
