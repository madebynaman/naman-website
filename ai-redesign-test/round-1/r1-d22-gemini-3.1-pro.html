My Philosophy

Consistency Beats Talent

I believe that consistent efforts toward your craft outpace raw talent. True mastery is the result of showing up and improving slightly every single day.

Let Curiosity Guide

Curiosity fuels an endless quest for understanding. Knowing a little about a lot helps connect the dots between scattered information to create meaningful work.

Form Follows Function

Problems are solved with functionality, not aesthetics. Yet, once the core problem is resolved, going the extra mile to craft beautiful products creates delight—and humans remember delight.

Give a Damn

An entrepreneurial mindset is essential. I take extreme ownership of every project, collaborating deeply with product and engineering to ensure success.
