Selected Stories
Asian Paints // B2B Supply Chain

Digitizing the dealer supply network

20% Increase in online transaction volume

Distributors and dealers often faced immense friction ordering stock. I revamped the multi-platform dealer portal to simplify transactions, reduce sales-rep bottlenecks, and boost engagement.

By introducing intelligent barcode scanning, personalized discount reminders, and size-based incentives directly in the UI, order speed dropped to under 10 seconds.

Role & Scope
UX Research, Journey Audits, UI Design. Managed with Thence agency.
Core Discovery
Dealers requested simple layout abstractions with role-specific clearance.

NowFloats // Boost360 onboarding

Mitigating onboarding drop-offs

60% Decrease in app uninstall rate

New users were uninstalling the mobile business portal app within 4 hours. By auditing acquisition funnels, I discovered a major mismatch between marketing promises and setup complexity.

The solution involved restructuring onboarding into highly visual quick wins—allowing small business owners to build their website in under two minutes during signup.

Role & Scope
UX Audits, Usability Testing, Visual Flows. Done at Thence.
Core Discovery
Eliminated non-mandatory steps to give users immediate setup wins.
Wikimedia Foundation // Field Research

Understanding KaiOS/JioPhone adopters

25 In-depth Field Interviews across India

JioPhone users wanted smart phone advantages (voice commands, cheap repairs, battery life) on keypad-driven feature phones. We audited reading behaviors on small screens for Wikipedia.

Key guidelines delivered included voice user interface search, regional translations prioritizing English digits, index tables, and media formatting guidelines optimized for 2.4" screens.

Role & Scope
Field Interviews, Personas, Journey Maps. Done at Hureo.
Core Discovery
Users prefer video and short paragraphs over massive text blocks on KaiOS.

Methodology

The 6-step iteration pipeline.

A structured feedback loop designed to align product targets with engineering constraints.

01
Research
Diving into the client brief, interviewing users, and building competitive matrices.
02
User Journey Mapping
Tracing step-by-step logic, personas, and expectations to reveal hidden user friction.
03
Design Exploration
Brainstorming wide wireframe solutions, mapping flows, and testing early layouts.
04
Refinement
Polishing type scales, spacing tokens, visual contrast, and high-fidelity interaction guidelines.
05
Handoff
Providing structured specifications to engineering and collaborating to enforce layout integrity.
06
Monitoring
Tracking analytics post-launch to continually polish conversion rates and remove friction.
