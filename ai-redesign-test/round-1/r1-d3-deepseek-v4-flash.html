How I Work

A process built from experience

Every step here earned its place. I've learned that skipping any of these creates problems downstream.

01

Research

Understanding the problem, the customer, and the competitive landscape before touching any tool. User interviews, competitive analysis, heuristic evaluation.

02

User Journey

Mapping the end-to-end experience to uncover friction. Personas, journey maps, decision points — finding where the experience breaks.

03

Design Exploration

Going wide before going deep. Exploring multiple directions, rapid prototyping, testing assumptions through collaborative critique.

04

Design Refinement

Polishing the chosen direction. Visual design, design system thinking, preparing documentation for handoff.

05

Handoff

Close collaboration with engineering and product to ensure faithful implementation. Not a throw-over-the-wall approach.

06

Monitor

Shipping is the beginning, not the end. Tracking metrics, monitoring performance, and iterating based on real usage data.
