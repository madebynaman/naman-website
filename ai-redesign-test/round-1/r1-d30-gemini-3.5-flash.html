Design Philosophy

Consistency compounds

I strongly believe that consistent efforts towards your craft are more important than talent. Everyday improvements compound into unimaginable results over time.

Curious generalist

Curiosity connects scattered insights. Holding wide knowledge across coding logic, technology systems, and finance helps me build highly adaptable frameworks.

Form follows function

Aesthetics alone do not solve user pain points. Once utility is solved, we apply visual polish to design beautiful experiences that create memorable delight.

Give a damn

High agency and a founder's mindset. I hold myself accountable for implementation outcomes and work hand-in-hand with development teams.

My Process

The Methodical Iteration Pipeline

1. Research
2. Journey
3. Explore
4. Refine
5. Handoff
6. Monitor

Deep-diving into constraints

I start by gathering context about target demographics, competitor strategies, and business requirements. I aim to uncover the core friction points before drafting a single canvas.

Primary Output
Audits & Competitor Matrices

Mapping user decisions

I build detailed step-by-step user expectation mappings and identify drop-off zones in existing architectures. This ensures we understand exactly where drop-offs occur.

Primary Output
Journey Maps & Personas

Going wide with solutions

I test multiple layouts, wireframe structures, and workflow permutations. Testing diverse layouts ensures we don't settle for conservative patterns.

Primary Output
Wireframes & UX Architecture

Hardening design tokens

I refine the chosen direction with strict typography scales, visual alignment tokens, and subtle interactive states to create delightful micro-experiences.

Primary Output
Hi-Fi UI Prototypes
