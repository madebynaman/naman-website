thesis.md — the practice in one paragraph 02 · read-only

I design products that users love, for brands that care. That's it.

Self-taught Product / UX designer — three years in UX on top of a computer-science background. Working remotely from Jaipur, India, for start-ups building 0→1 and enterprises that want UX taken seriously.

The work is research-rooted and built to be measured — improving people's lives with design and tech, one pixel at a time. This console is the record: problems, methods, and what the work actually achieved.

principles.conf — four standing constraints 03 · core
01 consistency = > talent Consistent effort compounds into results talent can't match on its own. Not the most talented designer — striving for consistency and constant improvement, every day.
02 curiosity :: guide A little about a lot of things, to connect dots between scattered information. Each project is discovery and exploration. Curiosity makes work that actually makes a difference.
03 form → function Problems are solved with functionality, not aesthetics. But once solved, the extra mile on beauty creates delight — and humans remember delight.
04 give_a_damn = true A founder's mindset, real ownership, collaboration as shared responsibility. High agency, and the willingness to go the extra mile to make the work succeed.

how might we improve the dealer experience — digitizing order placement & supply-chain tracking — while increasing transaction volumes and AOV?

Dealers didn't want to call a salesperson for every order. Every competitor already had a portal, and dealers were uniformly frustrated with all of them. Role-based abstraction was needed for users with varying privileges, and Asian Paints held demand data it could use to nudge dealers intelligently.

preview — dealer portal · catalog homefig.01
  • Heuristic evaluation and end-to-end redesign of the existing portal.
  • Personalized product recommendations from each dealer's order history.
  • Order-size incentives & loyalty points to lift AOV; data-driven regional promotions.
  • Order a product in under 10 seconds by scanning its barcode.

new users were uninstalling before they signed up. how might we reduce the uninstall rate and push users to complete onboarding, try the app, and stay?

Signup success sat near 20%. Time to signup averaged ~30 minutes. Eleven hypotheses — crashes, OTP delays, ad-driven expectation mismatch. Research narrowed it: the signup UX was poor, and marketing had promised something the app didn't quite deliver.

case.03 · NS/WIK/2020

wikipedia
kaios research

agency
Hureo
year
2020
role
UX research · personas · journey
with

field research25 pax3 cities

understand jioPhone users — a smart feature phone on kaiOS — and how they read, search, and understand wikipedia on a small keypad screen.

One-on-one, in-person interviews in Pune, Mumbai, and Delhi. 25 participants — students, business owners, homemakers, bank employees, salesmen, field executives, 18–40. 45–60 minutes each: 30–45 on background and habits, then observed task-based usage on their own devices.

preview — jioPhone article view, field-observedfig.03
  • Why JioPhone: smart features at affordable price, family Jio sims, cheap to repair, durable, familiar keypad, strong battery.
  • Wishlist: more images & video, English numerals in Hindi articles, in-article TOC, in-article search, latest-first ordering, bigger fonts.
  • Gap: users didn't know they could search inside Wikipedia, or that it existed in regional languages.
