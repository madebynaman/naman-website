Operating lanes

A process built for messy product rooms.

The method is straightforward because the situations are not: learn, map, explore, refine, hand off, monitor.

1Research

Understand the problem, possible solutions, and customer reality.

2User journey

Expose decision points, emotional friction, and behavior patterns.

3Exploration

Go wide with concepts and test the promising ones with collaborators.

4Refinement

Polish the chosen direction and document decisions.

5Handoff

Work closely with product and engineering so implementation keeps intent.

6Monitoring

Watch live performance and keep improving after launch.
