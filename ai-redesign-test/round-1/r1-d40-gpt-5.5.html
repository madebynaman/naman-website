How he works

The method is practical because the rooms are practical.

Naman's process is easy to understand, which is exactly the point. It gives clients confidence that ambiguity will move toward decisions.

From signal to shipped surface

  1. Research

    Understand the problem, customer, and possible solutions.

  2. User journey

    Map personas, decisions, friction, and moments that matter.

  3. Design exploration

    Go wide, test directions, iterate with collaborators.

  4. Design refinement

    Polish visual design and document why decisions were made.

  5. Handoff

    Work with product and engineering to protect intent.

  6. Monitoring

    Track performance and keep improving after launch.
