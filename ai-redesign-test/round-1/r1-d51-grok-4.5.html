Dealer Portal — digitize ordering without the sales call tax

How might we improve dealer experience by digitizing order placement and supply chain tracking while increasing transaction volumes and AOV?

  • Competitor portals exist — but dealers hate using all of them
  • Privilege abstraction and data-driven nudges were missing

Boost360 Thence · 2021 Research · Design · Usability testing

Onboarding — stop the uninstall before signup

Signup success sat at 20%. Average time to success: 30 minutes. Users left within 4 hours of install.

  • Root cause wasn’t crashes — it was expectation mismatch from ads
  • Signup UX was hard to understand; free vs paid unclear
  • Visual onboarding + quick wins + social proof rebuilt trust
60% decrease in uninstall rate
Signup time dropped significantly

Field research — JioPhone users and Wikipedia

Wikimedia wanted to understand JioPhone users: phone habits, internet use, Wikipedia awareness, and why feature phones still win.

  • 25 one-on-one interviews across Pune, Mumbai, Delhi
  • Motivation: affordable smart features, durability, keypad familiarity, battery
  • Recommendations spanned VUI, readability, regional SEO, and UI for small screens
