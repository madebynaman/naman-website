i.

On the record

A short, jargon-free account of how I think and why it works.

Design, for me, starts as a question, not a canvas. Before a single frame exists I want to know who is stuck, where, and why — because a beautiful screen that solves the wrong problem is just expensive decoration. I came to this work from computer engineering, which is a fancy way of saying I like understanding how things actually work before I try to make them better.

Three years in, across an agency and a bench of freelance clients, the pattern holds: talk to real users, find the friction, design the smallest change that moves the metric, then watch what happens after launch. I care about the number and the feeling — solve the problem first, then earn the delight.

What I don't do

Foster innovation by synergizing aesthetics and functionality, orchestrate design revolutions, and perpetuate narratives that transcend boundaries.

I hate jargon. I make hard things easy to use. That's it.

ii.

Four working beliefs

Not a manifesto — just the rules I actually design by.

01

Consistency beats talent

Talent is a nice head start. Showing up for the craft every single day is what compounds into work that lasts.

02

Let curiosity guide you

I know a little about a lot. That breadth is how I connect scattered signals into an insight nobody was looking for.

03

Form follows function

Problems are solved with functionality, not ornament. Once it works, beauty is the extra mile — and humans remember delight.

04

Give a damn

Founder's mindset, high agency, full ownership. Success is shared; I go the extra mile to get us there.

The dealer portal that paid for itself

Digitising order placement and supply-chain tracking for paint dealers who were tired of phoning a salesman for everything.

The problem

Dealers didn't want to call a rep for every order. Every competitor already had a portal — all of them frustrating. Asian Paints held demand data it wasn't using to nudge the right buy.

The move

  • End-to-end redesign after a full heuristic audit
  • 10-second reorder by scanning a product barcode
  • Recommendations & loyalty from order history
  • Data-driven promos tied to regional demand
+20%
increase in online transaction volume, plus a healthy lift in dealer engagement and loyalty.

Fixing an onboarding that lost users in four hours

People were uninstalling before they ever signed up. Only 1 in 5 got through — and it took them half an hour to fail.

The real reason

Not crashes or OTPs. Signup UX was confusing, and the app didn't deliver what the ads had promised. An expectation gap, dressed up as a technical one.

The move

  • Made signup visual and self-explanatory
  • Communicated the real USPs during onboarding
  • Built trust with social proof
  • Engineered quick wins — value before commitment
−60%
drop in the uninstall rate, with signup time cut dramatically — validated in usability testing.

Listening to the next billion readers

Why do people choose a JioPhone over a smartphone — and how does Wikipedia need to change to reach them?

The study

25 in-person interviews across three cities, with people aged 18–40 on smart feature phones. First the conversation, then watching them use their device on real tasks.

What we recommended

  • Voice-first interaction over the keypad
  • Readability for small screens, video over walls of text
  • Make in-article search discoverable
  • SEO for regional languages
25

iii.

How the work moves

Six stages. Linear on paper, looping in practice.

01

Research.

Understand the problem, learn the customer, and map the possible solutions before falling for any of them.

02

User Journey.

Personas, journeys and the real decision-making — where people hesitate, drop, and decide.

03

Design Exploration.

Go wide. Test, iterate, and collaborate through the problem instead of around it.

04

Design Refinement.

Refine one direction, polish the visual craft, and document every decision for handoff.

05

Handoff.

Work shoulder-to-shoulder with engineering and product so what ships matches what was designed.

06

Monitoring.

Live isn't done. Track the metrics that define success and keep improving from real behaviour.
