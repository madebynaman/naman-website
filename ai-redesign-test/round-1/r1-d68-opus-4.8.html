02 Boost360 / NowFloats
via Thence · 2021
Research · UX · Testing
−60%app uninstall rate

Onboarding that stopped losing people.

Users were uninstalling within four hours; only 1 in 5 finished signup, and it took them half an hour. The real cause wasn't technical — it was a promise the product didn't keep.

PlaceholderVisual signup — onboarding flow

I made signup visual and self-explanatory, communicated the real USPs during onboarding, added social proof, and engineered quick wins so value arrived before commitment. Usability testing confirmed the fix; uninstalls fell 60% and signup time dropped sharply.
