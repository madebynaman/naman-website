Scene 02

The Method

Six stages. Every project moves through this cycle — not always linearly, but always completely.

01
Research
Understanding the problem, the users, the market. Competitive analysis, user interviews, heuristic evaluation.
02
User Journey
Mapping personas, decision flows, and pain points. Finding the insights that will shape the solution.
03
Design Exploration
Going wide. Multiple directions, rapid prototyping, testing, iterating with product and engineering.
04
Design Refinement
Polishing the winner. Visual design, documentation, preparing every screen for handoff.
05
Handoff
Working alongside engineering to ensure pixel-perfect implementation.
06
Monitoring
Shipping is the midpoint, not the finish. Tracking metrics, verifying impact, iterating on live data.
