Sketch 01 — Thence / 2021

Asian Paints: Dealer Portal

UX Research · UX Design · UI Design
sketch: dealer portal — home, catalog, order flow

Dealers were stuck calling sales reps for every order. Every competitor had a portal, and all of them were painful. The fix? An end-to-end redesign with personalized recommendations based on order history, barcode scanning for 10-second reorders, and data-driven promotions tied to geographical trends and consumer demand.

20% increase in online transaction volume
Sketch 02 — Thence / 2021

Boost360: Onboarding Redesign

UX Research · UX Design · Usability Testing
sketch: onboarding flow — signup, value props, quick wins

Users were uninstalling within 4 hours of installing. Signup success was 20% at 30 minutes. Long hypothesis list — crashes, OTP delays, permissions. The real cause? Marketing promised one thing, onboarding delivered another. Made signup visual, communicated USPs upfront, built trust with social proof, and created quick wins so users saw value immediately.

60% decrease in app uninstall rate
Sketch 03 — Hureo / 2020

Wikipedia: KaiOS User Research

UX Research · User Personas · Journey Mapping
sketch: research setup — interview guide, persona cards, journey map

The Wikimedia Foundation wanted to understand JioPhone users. 25 in-person interviews across Pune, Mumbai, and Delhi — 45 to 60 minutes each. Users wanted voice-based search, video-format content, simpler Hindi words, English numerals for dates, and a table of contents for long articles.

Research informed Wikipedia's KaiOS strategy
Sketch 04 — Freelance / 2022–23

Turns: Brand & Landing Pages

Web Design · Brand Guidelines · Design System
sketch: brand board, landing page wireframes, component library

A startup needed more than a website refresh — they needed a multi-platform brand identity and a design system that could scale. Full UX audit, redesigned landing pages, established brand guidelines. The revamp directly increased conversions and became part of the investor pitch deck.
