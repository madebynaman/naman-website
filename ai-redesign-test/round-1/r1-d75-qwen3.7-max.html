Thence / 2021

Asian Paints: Dealer Portal

UX Research · UX Design · UI Design
case-study hero — dealer portal screens
The Problem

Dealers don't want to talk to a salesperson every time they need to place an order. Every competitor already has a digital portal — and dealers are frustrated with all of them. The existing portal needed different access levels for different user privileges, and Asian Paints had valuable trend data they weren't using to nudge dealers.

The Response
solution mockup — personalized recommendations, barcode scan

End-to-end heuristic evaluation and redesign of the multi-platform dealer portal. Introduced personalized product recommendations based on order history, data-driven promotions tied to geographical trends, periodic incentives to increase AOV, and a barcode scanner for 10-second reorders. Reduced dealer-salesman interaction and costs.

▲ 20% increase in online transaction volume
Thence / 2021

Boost360: Onboarding

UX Research · UX Design · Usability Testing
case-study hero — onboarding flow screens
The Problem

New users are uninstalling within 4 hours of installation. Signup success rate is 20% with a 30-minute average completion time. The hypothesis list was long — crashes, OTP delays, permissions, ads, language barriers. But the real cause was more subtle than anyone expected.

The Response
solution mockup — visual signup, quick wins, social proof

The root cause: marketing promised one thing, onboarding delivered another. Made signup visual and easy to understand. Communicated app USPs during onboarding. Built trust with social proof. Created quick wins so users saw results immediately. Identified the communication mismatch across all marketing touchpoints.

▲ 60% decrease in app uninstall rate
Hureo / 2020

Wikipedia: KaiOS Research

UX Research · Personas · Journey Mapping
research artifact — interview setup, personas
The Question

The Wikimedia Foundation wanted to understand JioPhone users — people using smart feature phones running KaiOS. Who are they? Why a feature phone over a smartphone? What do they need from Wikipedia that they're not getting?

The Findings
research output — journey maps, recommendations

25 one-on-one in-person interviews across Pune, Mumbai, and Delhi. 45–60 minute sessions. Users wanted voice-based interaction, video-format content, simpler Hindi, English numerals for dates, and a table of contents for long articles. Research informed Wikipedia's KaiOS product strategy.

● 25 interviews across 3 cities

Methodology

How the Conversation Flows

1

Research

Understanding the problem space, considering possible solutions, and learning about the customer before touching any design tool.

2

User Journey

Diving deep into user journeys to uncover insights. Exploring personas, mapping decision-making, understanding the full context.

3

Design Exploration

Going wide with a range of solutions. Testing, iterating, collaborating, and working through the problem at hand.

4

Design Refinement

Polishing the chosen direction, refining visual design, preparing for handoff, and documenting every decision made.

5

Handoff

Working closely with engineering and product to ensure a successful handoff and effective implementation.

6

Monitoring

Once live, the work isn't done. Tracking performance and metrics that indicate success — and iterating from there.
