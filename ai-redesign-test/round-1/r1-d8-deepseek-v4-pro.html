Process

Six stages. Every project moves through this cycle — not always linearly, but always completely.
01
Research
Understanding the problem, the users, the market. Competitive analysis, user interviews, heuristic evaluation of existing interfaces.
02
User Journey
Diving deep into the user journey. Exploring personas, mapping decision flows, and uncovering the insights that shape the solution.
03
Design Exploration
Going wide. Exploring a range of possible solutions, testing, iterating, collaborating with product and engineering.
04
Design Refinement
Refining the chosen direction. Polishing visual design, preparing for handoff, documenting decisions made.
05
Handoff
Working closely with engineering and product to ensure successful implementation of designs as intended.
06
Monitoring
Once live, monitoring performance and tracking metrics that indicate success. Shipping is the midpoint, not the finish.

Operating Principles

What guides the work when the brief is ambiguous and the stakes are real.

Consistency beats talent

Consistent effort always compounds into unimaginable results. I'm not the most talented designer in the world, but I strive for consistency and constant improvement — everyday. It's the single variable I can control.

Form follows function

Problems are solved with functionality, not aesthetics. At the core of every good product is how it solves for the needs of its users. Once the problem is solved, going the extra mile and designing beautiful products creates delight. And humans remember delight.

Let curiosity guide you

Curiosity fuels an endless quest for understanding. I know a little about a lot of things — it helps me connect the dots between scattered information. Each project is a journey of discovery, constant learning, and exploration.

Give a damn

I'm highly entrepreneurial and strive to have a founder's mindset. Success is a shared responsibility that is an outcome of effective collaboration. I take ownership for each project I work on. I'm committed to going the extra mile to achieve success.
