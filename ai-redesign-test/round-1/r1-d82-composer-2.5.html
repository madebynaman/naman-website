Study 01 — Asian Paints Dealer Portal Thence · 2021

Problem statement

How might we improve dealer UX by digitizing order placement and supply chain tracking while increasing transaction volumes and AOV?

Key finding: Every competitor has a digital portal, but dealers are frustrated with all of them. Opportunity: be the one they don't hate.

Solution: Heuristic evaluation, end-to-end redesign, barcode ordering in <10 seconds, personalized recommendations, role-based access.

Study 02 — Boost360 Onboarding Thence · 2021

Problem statement

20% signup success rate. Users uninstall within 4 hours. Average signup time: 30 minutes.

Root cause: Poor signup UX + mismatch between marketing communication and app reality.

Solution: Visual signup flow, USP communication during onboarding, social proof, quick wins, in-app support access.

Study 03 — Wikipedia KaiOS Research Hureo · 2020

Methodology

25 one-on-one, in-person interviews across Pune, Mumbai, Delhi. 45–60 min sessions: background interview + device observation tasks.

Q: Why JioPhone over smartphone? A: Smart features at affordable price. Durable. Good battery. Familiar keypad. Q: What would improve Wikipedia? A: Voice search. Bigger fonts. Videos. Table of contents. Regional language SEO.

Deliverables: Personas, journey maps, ecosystem maps, research report with recommendations for voice UI and readability optimization.

Research artifact — participant persona card, journey map

process doc

Methodology

Six-stage design process

  1. Research — Understanding the problem, solutions, and customer.
  2. User journey — Personas, journeys, decision-making insights.
  3. Design exploration — Going wide, testing, iterating, collaborating.
  4. Design refinement — Polishing visuals, documenting for handoff.
  5. Handoff — Close collaboration with engineering and product.
  6. Monitoring — Tracking success metrics post-launch.

Operating principles
