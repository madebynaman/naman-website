process

// Six stages. Not always linear, always complete.

[01] Research
Understanding the problem, the users, the market. Competitive analysis, user interviews, heuristic evaluation.
[02] User Journey
Mapping personas, decision flows, and pain points. Uncovering insights that will shape the solution direction.
[03] Design Exploration
Going wide: multiple solutions, rapid prototyping, testing, iterating with product and engineering.
[04] Design Refinement
Polishing the chosen direction. Visual design, documentation, preparing for handoff.
[05] Handoff
Working alongside engineering to ensure pixel-perfect implementation of designs as intended.
[06] Monitoring
Once live, tracking metrics to verify impact. Shipping is the midpoint, not the finish line.
