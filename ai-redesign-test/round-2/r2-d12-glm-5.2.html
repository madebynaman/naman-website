The Process

06 phases / p. 06

No two projects follow the exact same path, but every project starts with research and ends with monitoring. This is the backbone.

01

Research

Understand the problem, consider possible solutions, and learn the customer. I don't start drawing until I know who I'm drawing for.

02

User Journey

Dive deep into the journey to uncover insights. Personas, journeys, and decision-making at every touchpoint.

03

Exploration

Go wide and explore a range of solutions. Test, iterate, collaborate — work the problem without premature convergence.

04

Refinement

Refine the chosen direction and polish the visual design. Prepare for handoff and document the decisions made along the way.

05

Handoff

Work closely with engineering and product to ensure successful implementation. A design is only as good as its execution.

06

Monitoring

Once live, the work isn't done. Monitor performance and track metrics that indicate success — and find the next thing to improve.

Principles

04 beliefs / p. 08

These aren't posters on a wall. They're heuristics I use when I'm stuck, when the path forward is unclear, or when I need to decide what matters.

P-01

Consistency beats talent

I'm not the most talented designer in the world, but I strive for consistency and constant improvement — everyday. Consistency compounds into unimaginable results.

P-02

Let curiosity guide you

Curiosity fuels my endless quest for understanding. I know a little about a lot of things — it helps me connect the dots between scattered information.

P-03

Form follows function

Problems are solved with functionality, not aesthetics. But once the problem is solved, going the extra mile creates delight. Humans remember delight.

P-04

Give a damn

I'm highly entrepreneurial and strive to have a founder's mindset. Success is a shared responsibility. I take ownership for each project I work on.
