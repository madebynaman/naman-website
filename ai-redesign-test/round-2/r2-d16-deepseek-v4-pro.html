DESIGN_PRINCIPLES 0x003

01Consistency beats talent

I'm not the most talented designer alive. I show up every day instead, and that compounds. Designing since 2017, no year off.

02Curiosity is the job

Knowing a little about a lot connects scattered dots. The Wikipedia study shaped recommendations more than anything I knew about interface design.

03Form follows function

Function solves the problem. Beauty is what people remember, and you earn the second by doing the first. Fix what's broken, then make it feel good.

04Give a damn

Founder's mindset. Full ownership. At Turns, that meant building a design system nobody asked for so the team could ship after I left. No drama, high agency.
