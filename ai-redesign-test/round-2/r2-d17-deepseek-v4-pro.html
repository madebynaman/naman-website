03 / How it gets doneSix steps
1

Research

Understand the problem and the customer before drawing anything.

2

Journey

Map the full experience. Find where it breaks.

3

Explore

Wide first. Ten bad directions beats one early commitment.

4

Refine

Pick the winner. Document every decision.

5

Ship

Stay with engineering until what ships matches the design.

6

Monitor

Live ≠ done. Watch the numbers. Fix what didn't move.

04 / BeliefsWhat I design by

Consistency
beats talent.

I'm not the most talented designer alive. I show up every day instead, and that compounds. Designing since 2017, in UX since 2019, 15+ clients, no year off.

Curiosity
is the job.

Knowing a little about a lot connects scattered dots. The Wikipedia study proved this: understanding why someone picks a keypad phone shaped the recommendations more than anything I knew about interface design.

Form follows
function.

Function solves the problem. Beauty is what people remember, and you earn the second by doing the first. On Boost360, no visual polish would have moved the number until research found the real problem.

Give a
damn.

Founder's mindset, full ownership. At Turns, that meant building brand guidelines and a design system nobody asked for, so the team could keep shipping after I left.
