04 · What I believe

Consistency beats talent.

I'm not the most talented designer. I show up every day instead, and that compounds. Designing since 2017, in UX since 2019 — no year off.

Curiosity is the job.

Knowing a little about a lot connects scattered dots. The Wikipedia study proved this: understanding why someone picks a keypad phone shaped the recommendations more than interface knowledge.

Form follows function.

Function solves. Beauty makes people remember. Earn the second by doing the first. On Boost360, no visual polish would have moved the number until research found the real problem.

Give a damn.

Founder's mindset. Full ownership. At Turns, that meant building brand guidelines and a design system nobody asked for, so the team could keep shipping after I left.
