04

What I believe.

Belief 01

Consistency beats talent.

I show up every day. Designing since 2017, in UX since 2019, 15+ clients, no year off. Compounding effort outperforms raw talent.

Belief 02

Curiosity is the job.

Knowing a little about a lot connects scattered dots. The Wikipedia study proved this: understanding why someone picks a keypad phone shaped the recommendations more than interface knowledge.

Belief 03

Form follows function.

Function solves. Beauty earns memory. You do the second by doing the first. On Boost360, no visual polish could have moved the metric until research found the real problem.

Belief 04

Give a damn.

Founder's mindset. Full ownership. At Turns, that meant building brand guidelines and a design system nobody asked for, so the team could ship after I left. No drama, high agency.
