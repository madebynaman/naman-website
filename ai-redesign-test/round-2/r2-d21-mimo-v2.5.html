How I work

6 steps · order matters
01

Listen

Users, founders, analytics, support tickets. Most of what I need already exists inside the company.

02

Map

The full journey, end to end, until I can see where it breaks.

03

Explore

Go wide before narrowing. I would rather throw away ten directions than defend the first one.

04

Refine

Pick a direction and sweat the details. Document every decision.

05

Ship

Stay with engineering through the build, screen by screen, until it's live.

06

Monitor

Once live, the work isn't done. Track metrics. Fix what moved, and fix what didn't.

Principles

4 rules · non-negotiable
01

Consistency beats talent

I'm not the most talented designer alive. I show up every day instead, and that compounds. Designing since 2017, in UX since 2019, 15+ clients, no year off.

02

Curiosity is the job

I know a little about a lot, which helps me connect things other people keep separate. Understanding why someone picks a keypad phone shaped the Wikipedia work more than anything I knew about interfaces.

03

Form follows function

Function solves the problem. Beauty is what makes people remember it, and you earn the second by doing the first. On a signup rescue, no amount of visual polish moved the number until research found the real problem.

04

Give a damn

I work with a founder's mindset and take ownership of what I touch. At Turns that meant brand guidelines and a design system so the team could keep shipping after I left. Nobody asked for that part.
