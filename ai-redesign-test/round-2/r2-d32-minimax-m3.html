// studios/asian-paints · 2021 · th3nce

The dealer portal, reconsidered.

type
commerce · b2b portal
role
ux research, ux, ui
team
ui · pm · dm · client
stack
figma · principle · miro

Dealers wanted to order without calling a salesperson every time. Every competitor had a portal, and dealers hated all of them. The redesign brought role-based access, intelligent recommendations from order history, stock checks, loyalty incentives, order tracking, and a barcode flow that put an order in the cart in under ten seconds.

┌─────────────────────────────┐
│ HOME   │  TRENDING  │  CART(3) │
├─────────────────────────────┤
│ ▦ Deals near you      │

├─────────────────────────────┤
│ [SCAN ▣] [CATALOG] [ORDERS] │
└─────────────────────────────┘

Heuristic review first, then end-to-end redesign of the existing portal. New features reduced the dealer's dependency on a salesperson and, with it, the cost that came with every call. Barcode scan ordering made the cart reachable in under 10 seconds.

// studios/boost360 · 2021 · th3nce

Make the first win visible.

type
activation · mobile onboarding
role
research, ux, ui, usability
team
ui · motion · pm · dm
stack
figma · protopie · principle

New users installed the app and left before signing up. Uninstalls inside four hours. Signup success at 20%, average time to a successful signup at 30 minutes. Research traced the cause to a single gap: marketing promised one thing, signup delivered another. The new flow visualised the process, built trust with social proof, and put a working website in front of the user before they could lose interest.

╭───────────────────╮
│  01  │ See how it works  │
│  02  │ Pick a category    │
│  03  │ Create your site   │
│  04  │ Publish & share   │
╰───────────────────╯
  ↓ live in < 4 min

Visual, self-explanatory signup that communicated app USPs during onboarding rather than after. Social proof inside the flow, quick wins before the user could lose interest, and a direct line to support for anyone stuck. Usability testing showed the new flow landed every time.

−60%
drop in app uninstalls in the first two weeks
// research/wikipedia-kaios · 2020 · hur3o

Knowledge on a feature phone.

type
field research · 25 sessions
role
recruit · interview · synthesis
team
founders · sr. researchers
client
Wikimedia Foundation

Twenty-five in-person sessions across Pune, Mumbai and Delhi with JioPhone users — for many of them the only internet-connected device in the house. First an interview on background, reading habits, phone and internet use, then observed tasks on the participant's own device. The research surfaced needs the Wikipedia team hadn't seen yet: voice input, English numerals, a table of contents, video content, and search they couldn't miss.

Pune      ●●●●●●●  8
Mumbai    ●●●●●●    6
Delhi     ●●●●●●●●●●  11
─────────────────────────────────
total     25 sessions · 3 cities
45-60 min each

Findings went to the Wikimedia Foundation as personas, journey maps, ecosystem maps and a full research report. The most useful recommendation was the one that wasn't about Wikipedia at all: voice input. Typing on a keypad is the real barrier to information access on a feature phone.

process.sh

how the work gets made

process.sh
→ step 01

listen

Users, founders, analytics, support tickets. Most of what I need already exists inside the company. It just isn't in one place yet. #research #interviews #analytics

→ step 02

map

The full journey, end to end, until I can see where it breaks. Personas when they're honest. Journey maps when they're load-bearing. #ux #personas #flows

→ step 03

explore

Go wide before narrowing. I'd rather throw away ten directions than defend the first one. Sketches on paper, options in Figma, prototypes in code. #sketch #wireframe #prototype

→ step 04

refine

Pick a direction and sweat the details. Type, spacing, motion, system. The product earns its finish by doing the job first. #ui #system #motion

→ step 05

ship

Stay with engineering through the build, screen by screen, until it's live. Read the codebase when it helps. Translate when it doesn't. #handoff #qa #code

→ step 06

check

Watch the numbers afterwards. Fix what moved, fix what didn't. The job is not done because the build is live. #analytics #iteration

beliefs.toml

four things I design by

beliefs.toml
[principle.consistency]

consistency_beats_talent = true

I'm not the most talented designer in the room. I show up every day instead, and that compounds. Designing since 2017, in UX since 2019, fifteen plus clients, no year off.

[principle.curiosity]

curiosity = "the job"

I know a little about a lot, which helps me connect things other people keep separate. The Wikipedia study shaped its recommendations because we understood why someone picks a keypad phone over a smartphone.

[principle.function]

form_follows_function = true

Function solves the problem. Beauty is what makes people remember it, and you earn the second by doing the first. On the Boost360 onboarding, no visual polish would have moved the number until research found the real problem.

[principle.ownership]

give_a_damn = "always"

Founder's mindset. Take ownership past the assigned frame. At Turns that meant handing the team brand guidelines and a design system so they could keep shipping consistently after I left. Nobody asked for it.
