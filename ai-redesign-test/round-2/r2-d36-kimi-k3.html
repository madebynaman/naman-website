DRG NO. NS-AP-2021-AASIAN PAINTS · THENCE · 2021
UX Research · UX Design · UI Design

Dealer portal built around how dealers actually order

Dealers didn't want to phone a salesperson for every order, and every competing portal frustrated them. Heuristic evaluation, then an end-to-end redesign: barcode reordering in under 10 seconds, recommendations from order history, and promotions driven by regional demand data.

DRG NO. NS-B3-2021-BBOOST360 · THENCE · 2021
UX Research · UX Design · UI Design · Usability Testing

Onboarding that closes the expectation gap

Users uninstalled within four hours; signup success sat at 20% and took 30 minutes. Twelve hypotheses tested, real culprits found: a confusing signup and a gap between ad promises and the app. The redesign made onboarding visual, built trust with social proof, and engineered a quick win: your business website, live in minutes.

03

Assembly sequence

The same six operations, in order, every time. Skipping a step voids the warranty.

OP-1

Research

Understand the problem, the customer, and the possible solutions before anything is drawn.

OP-2

User journey

Map personas, journeys and decision-making to surface the insights that matter.

OP-3

Exploration

Go wide. Test, iterate, collaborate. Work through the problem, not around it.

OP-4

Refinement

Polish the chosen direction, document decisions, prepare for handoff.

OP-5

Handoff

Work shoulder-to-shoulder with engineering and product so ships matches designed.

OP-6

Monitoring

Live isn't done. Track the metrics that define success and keep improving.

04

Material certifications

Four operating principles, tested to destruction. Certificates held since day one.

CERT NS-01 · COMPOUNDING

Consistency beats talent

Consistent effort compounds into unimaginable results. Not the most talented designer in the world, just the one who shows up and improves, every day.

CERT NS-02 · NAVIGATION

Let curiosity guide you

Knowing a little about a lot of things is how scattered information becomes connected dots. Every project is a journey of discovery.

CERT NS-03 · FUNCTION FIRST

Form follows function

Solve the need first. Then go the extra mile and make it beautiful, because humans remember delight.

CERT NS-04 · OWNERSHIP

Give a damn

Founder's mindset, high agency, shared responsibility. Ownership of every project, committed to the extra mile.
