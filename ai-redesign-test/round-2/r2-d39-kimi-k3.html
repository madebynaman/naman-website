v3.2.0 Boost360: onboarding overhaul THENCE · 2021
UX RESEARCH · UX · UI · USABILITY TESTING

New users uninstalled within 4 hours; signup success sat at 20% and took 30 minutes. Twelve hypotheses tested; real culprits: a confusing signup and an expectation gap set by ads. The redesign made onboarding visual, added social proof, and shipped a quick win: your business website, live in minutes.

−fix(onboarding): uninstall rate (−60%)
−fix(signup): average time to success (30 min → minutes)
+feat(onboarding): visual steps, social proof, support access
v3.1.0 Asian Paints: dealer portal THENCE · 2021
UX RESEARCH · UX · UI DESIGN

Dealers didn't want to phone a salesperson for every order — and every competing portal frustrated them. Heuristic evaluation, then an end-to-end redesign: barcode reordering in under 10 seconds, recommendations from order history, loyalty mechanics, region-wise demand-driven promotions.

+perf(portal): online transaction volume +20%
+feat(orders): barcode reorder in <10 seconds
−fix(sales-flow): dealer–salesman dependency reduced
v2.0.0 Wikipedia × KaiOS: field research HUREO · 2020
UX RESEARCH · PERSONAS · JOURNEY MAPPING

For the Wikimedia Foundation: 25 in-person interviews across Pune, Mumbai and Delhi with JioPhone users: why millions choose a smart feature phone, and how they actually read on one. Findings became recommendations: voice interfaces, readability, regional-language discovery.

+research(field): 25 participants · 3 cities · 45–60 min sessions
+docs(recs): voice UI, readability, regional SEO for Wikipedia
·note: ground truth only exists face to face
