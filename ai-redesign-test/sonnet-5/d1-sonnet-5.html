Selected work

1Asian Paints — Dealer Portal
Case-study hero — dealer portal redesign
(home / catalog / order-tracking screens)
Thence · 2021 · UX Research, UX Design, UI Design

Digitizing the dealer's ordering habit

Dealers were placing every order through a salesperson because the existing portals were unusable — and every competitor already had a digital channel their dealers actually used. We rebuilt the experience end to end: personalised product recommendations from order history, loyalty incentives tied to order size, and a barcode flow that gets a repeat order placed in under 10 seconds.

20%Increase in online transaction volume
↑Engagement & dealer loyalty
Reviewer's note — solved a trust problem before a UI problem: dealers didn't distrust the screens, they distrusted ordering without a human.
2Boost360 — Onboarding
Case-study hero — onboarding flow
(signup / OTP / first-win screens)
Thence · NowFloats · 2021 · Research, UX/UI Design, Usability Testing

Finding why users left before they'd even started

The app was being uninstalled within four hours of install, and only 1 in 5 signups succeeded. The obvious suspects — bugs, OTP delay, load time — weren't it. The real cause was a promise gap: marketing sold one thing, onboarding delivered another. We rebuilt the flow around visible quick wins, communicated the app's actual value honestly, and let non-mandatory steps be skipped.

60%Decrease in app uninstall rate
↓↓Time-to-signup, usability-tested
Reviewer's note — the fix wasn't a screen, it was a discipline: audit what marketing promises before redesigning what product delivers.
3Wikipedia — KaiOS Research
Field-research artifact — personas &
journey maps, JioPhone users
Hureo · 2020 · UX Research, Personas, Journey Mapping

Understanding Wikipedia through a JioPhone

The Wikimedia Foundation wanted to understand how JioPhone owners — an audience choosing affordability, durability, and a familiar keypad over a smartphone — actually read and searched Wikipedia. 25 face-to-face interviews across Pune, Mumbai and Delhi surfaced a clear brief: optimise for voice input, shrink dense paragraphs, and make search and regional-language content easier to find.

25Participants, 3 cities, 45–60 min sessions
6Concrete product recommendations delivered
Reviewer's note — no screens shipped here, just evidence. Included because it's where the research instincts on record actually come from.

Exhibit B

Process, on the record

Six stages, followed in order, on every engagement — because skipping straight to visuals is how portfolios full of decoration and short on decisions get made.

01

Research

Understand the problem, the customer, and what's already been tried.

02

User journey

Map personas, journeys, and where decisions actually get made.

03

Design exploration

Go wide on possible solutions; test, iterate, collaborate.

04

Design refinement

Polish the chosen direction and document the decisions behind it.

05

Handoff

Work with engineering and product until it ships as designed.

06

Monitoring

Live isn't done — track the metrics that prove the work worked.
